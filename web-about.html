About

Who We Are

The Hostel Entrepreneur Association of Nepal (HEAN) was founded in 2079 with the mission to unite hostel owners and entrepreneurs throughout Nepal. As a collective, we are dedicated to improving the standards of hostels, ensuring that they meet the evolving needs of students, professionals, and travelers alike. Our association provides a platform for collaboration, support, and shared growth, empowering our members to enhance their businesses and contribute to the hostel industry’s development. At HEAN, we believe that hostels play a crucial role in offering affordable and safe accommodations across the country. We work to establish best practices, ethical guidelines, and high service standards, helping hostel owners create environments where guests feel comfortable and at home. By fostering a spirit of innovation and continuous learning, we aim to uplift the entire sector.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a unified network of hostels across Nepal that embody the highest standards of safety, hospitality, and comfort. We envision a future where every hostel under our association is recognized for offering a home-like environment, ensuring peace of mind for students, travelers, and long-term residents alike. Our goal is to make Nepal a preferred destination for travelers, particularly students and budget-conscious visitors, by providing reliable, affordable, and welcoming hostel accommodations. We strive to elevate the image of the hostel industry in Nepal, ensuring that hostels are not just places of shelter but spaces that foster community and cultural exchange. By promoting industry best practices and advocating for improved standards, our vision is to see every hostel in Nepal meet international expectations in terms of cleanliness, security, and service.

Our Mission

The mission of Hostel Association Nepal is to empower hostel owners by providing the tools, resources, and support they need to excel in the competitive hospitality industry. We are dedicated to representing the interests of hostel owners, ensuring their voices are heard at both governmental and institutional levels. Through active advocacy, we aim to influence policy and create favorable conditions that help hostels thrive in an ever-evolving marketplace. Our mission is to foster a culture of continuous improvement within the hostel industry. We provide hostel owners with access to training, workshops, and educational resources to help them enhance their management skills, implement best practices, and deliver exceptional service to their guests. We are committed to ensuring that all hostels under our association uphold the highest standards of cleanliness, security, and customer satisfaction. In addition, we work to bridge the gap between hostel owners and the wider tourism and education sectors by establishing partnerships with local universities, tourism boards, and international travel agencies.

Our Goals

🤝
Unite hostel entrepreneurs across Nepal, creating a network that supports the sharing of knowledge and resources.
🏆
Promote the development of well-managed, high-quality hostels that meet the needs of different demographics.
📚
Provide training, guidance, and resources to help hostel owners improve their management, marketing, and operational skills.
📢
Advocate for the interests of hostel entrepreneurs at both the local and national levels, ensuring their voices are heard in policies affecting the industry.
