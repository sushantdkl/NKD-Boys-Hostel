About

Who We Are

HEAN (Hostel Entrepreneur Association of Nepal) is a non-profit organization dedicated to empowering hostel entrepreneurs, students, and communities across Nepal. We work to elevate standards, foster growth, and build the future of student housing.

✅ Standardization of Hostels
✅ Student Welfare Programs
✅ Entrepreneur Training
✅ Policy Advocacy
